Mitsugen

3D Printing — slice-based generation

A distinct generation pipeline from 3di-men's CAD engine. Plain bodies of revolution (a vase, a bottle, a bowl, a handle-less cup) go through a structured shape spec and a bounded sequence of construction slices, lofted into a real solid. Anything else — a mug with a handle, a toy car, a bracket, any object with real features or a non-round cross-section — is approximated through primitive composition (box/cylinder/sphere/cone + boolean ops) by a local LLM. Both paths are disclosed explicitly in the result; neither is claimed to be a perfect reproduction of a free-form object.
