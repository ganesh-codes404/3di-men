HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, all of them real.

  1. 01

    Describe

    Write what you want in plain language — dimensions if you have them, a rough description if you don't. If the request is underspecified, 3di-men asks a few clarifying questions before building anything.

  2. 02

    Generate

    3di-men splits your description into the separate physical parts it implies, then builds each one in parallel — entirely on your own machine, with nothing sent to the cloud or billed per request. On a laptop with no dedicated GPU, that step currently takes several minutes; the workspace shows a live timer so it's clear it's working, not stuck.

  3. 03

    Inspect

    Rotate, zoom, and check the result before you trust it — vertex and face counts, and whether it's watertight (so you know it'll actually slice and print, not just look right on screen). Download it as an STL, or open the manual tool console to tweak it by hand.

CAPABILITIES

What you can actually build.

Every row below is something you can build today, whether you describe it in a prompt or construct it by hand in the tool console.

CategoryWhat's supported
Primitives Box, cylinder, sphere, torus, capsule, ellipsoid, dome, ring, gear profile, revolve, shouldered cylinder, polygon frustum, and extruded 2D profiles (rectangle, rounded rectangle, L-shape).
Deformation & transforms Taper, flare, twist, bend, translate, rotate, mirror, scale.
Boolean combination Union, difference, and intersection between any two parts.
Holes & features Drilled holes (single and patterned), counterbores, slots, ribs, bosses, hex sockets, knurl patterns.
Edge & face editing Fillet and chamfer edges, push/pull faces, inset pockets, and box-edge rounding — directly in the 3D viewport, by dragging.
Assembly Independent parts combined by position offset — a gear and its shaft stay two parts, never force-fused into one mesh.
Prompt understanding On-device decomposition of a request into buildable parts, with clarifying questions for underspecified prompts.
Export STL.

No simulation, structural analysis, or additional export formats yet — what's listed above is what ships today.
